# Return Policy

## Standard window

Most products can be returned within 30 days of purchase when the item is unused or opened only for inspection.

## Defective items

Defective products can be returned within 60 days of purchase. The support team may approve refund, replacement, or repair depending on the category and stock status.

## Damaged on arrival

Items damaged during shipping can be refunded or replaced within 14 days of delivery. Evidence should be requested when the item value is above 100.

## Apparel

Apparel can be returned within 45 days when unused, unworn, and in resaleable condition.

## Electronics

Electronics must be returned within 30 days unless the customer reports a defect. Used electronics returned for preference reasons should be escalated.

## Abuse signals

Requests should be escalated when the customer has four or more returns in the last 90 days, when a high value item is used, or when the request is outside the policy window.
